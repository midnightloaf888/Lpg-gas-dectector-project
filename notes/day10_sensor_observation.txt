Day 10 - Sensor ADC Reading Observation
During normal conditions, the sensor readings remained mostly between approximately 250 and 270, corresponding to an estimated voltage of around 0.20 V to 0.22 V.
When the sensor was exposed to a stronger stimulus, the ADC value increased noticeably and then gradually decreased again. 
Some readings reached much higher values, including values above 1500 and, in one instance, the maximum ADC value of 4095.
This confirmed that the ESP32 was successfully reading changes in the sensor's analog output. 
The readings also showed natural fluctuations, which will be reduced using filtering and averaging during Day 11.
