Day 16 - Alarm Validation with LCD

Today I worked on integrating the LCD with my existing gas sensor setup for alarm validation.
The setup took quite a bit of time because I wasn’t using an I2C LCD. 
So I had to connect all the LCD pins individually and make sure there were no pin conflicts with the sensor, LED and buzzer.
After completing the setup, I tested the system today and it worked perfectly. 
The sensor readings and alarm status shown on the serial monitor were also being displayed correctly on the LCD.
This helped me confirm that the LCD was properly integrated with the complete alarm setup.
