Day 14 - Alarm Logic Validation

Objective:
To integrate the gas sensor, LED and buzzer into a complete gas leak detection system. 
The system continuously monitors the gas sensor, compares the ADC value with predefined thresholds and activates the appropriate alarm outputs.

Implementation:
The ESP32 reads the TGS2610 sensor every 100 ms and classifies the gas concentration into three states:

NORMAL
WARNING
DANGER

The alarm logic is as follows:

normal  - LED OFF, Buzzer OFF
warning - LED ON, Buzzer OFF
danger - LED ON, Buzzer ON

The sensor is read continuously for quick response, while the ADC value, voltage and system status are displayed on the Serial Monitor every 2 seconds for easier observation.

Observations:
Multiple trials were carried out to obtain consistent and accurate sensor readings.
During testing, it was observed that although the smell of gas was noticeable, the buzzer was not activating because the initial threshold values were set too high.

To improve the detector's response, the threshold values were adjusted based on the experimental readings:

Warning Threshold: Changed from 800 ADC to 700 ADC
Danger Threshold: Changed from 1500 ADC to 1350 ADC

After updating these threshold values, the system responded more accurately to gas exposure.
The LED and buzzer activated at the expected gas concentrations, making the detector more reliable during testing.

Conclusion:
The complete alarm system was successfully implemented and validated. 
The ESP32 was able to continuously monitor the gas sensor, determine the appropriate system state and control the LED and buzzer accordingly.
This marks the first fully integrated version of the gas leak detection system.
