Day 15 - LCD Basics

Today I worked on understanding the basics of the 16x2 LCD and tried to display a simple message on it.
I faced a few issues while setting it up, and it took me around two days to get it working properly.
 At first, I realised that I needed a potentiometer to control the LCD contrast.
Even after adding it, I was only seeing rectangle boxes and later some random characters on the display.
 I checked the wiring and made a few changes to the LCD initialization code.
After some trial and error, I was finally able to successfully display “Hello Aaryani!” on the LCD.
