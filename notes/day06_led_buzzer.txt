Day 6 - LED + Buzzer Combined Logic

Today I integrated the LED and buzzer into a single program to understand how multiple output devices can be controlled simultaneously using the ESP32.
The LED was connected to GPIO4 through a current-limiting resistor, while the buzzer was connected to GPIO5. Both devices shared a common ground connection.
I configured both GPIO pins as output pins and used gpio_set_level to switch them ON and OFF together. 
A delay of two seconds was added between each state change to clearly observe the synchronized operation.
This task demonstrated how multiple peripherals can be controlled within the same application.
It also provided a foundation for implementing the alarm logic of the LPG Gas Leak Detector, where visual (LED) and audible (buzzer) alerts must activate together whenever a hazardous gas concentration is detected.
